Logistics & Delivery 🚚

Shipping Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

We are committed to delivering your pet supplies safely and on time. Please review our shipping guidelines below.

1. Processing Time

All orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.

2. Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery timelines depend on your location and our logistics partners:

  • Metro Cities: 3–5 Business Days
  • Non-Metro Cities: 5–7 Business Days
  • Remote Areas: 7–10 Business Days

3. Third-Party Logistics

While we strive for timely delivery, The Pet Portal is not responsible for delays caused by third-party courier services, weather conditions, or unforeseen circumstances beyond our control.

For shipment tracking inquiries, please visit your Order Tracking Page.
