Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At The Pet Portal, we strive to ensure every pet and product finds a happy home. However, we understand that sometimes things don't go as planned.

1. Order Cancellation

Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: You can cancel your order at any time before it has been shipped. Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled.

2. Refund Process

Online Payments: If you are eligible for a refund (e.g., due to non-delivery or damaged goods), the refund will be processed back to your original payment method.

  • Processing time: 5–7 working days from the date of approval.
  • Refunds are subject to verification by the seller to ensure the claim is valid.

3. Verification

All refund requests are subject to seller verification. We may request images or videos of damaged products before processing a claim.

For any billing questions, please contact support@petportal.com.
